Common questions

Adobe & AEM, answered.

Is Aventis Technology an Adobe partner?

Yes. Aventis Technology is an Adobe partner delivering Adobe Experience Cloud implementations across Asia-Pacific, with a delivery organisation weighted approximately 70% toward Adobe-specialised roles including AEM architects, AEP consultants and Journey Optimizer leads.

Which Adobe products does Aventis Technology implement?

Adobe Experience Manager (Sites and Assets), Adobe Experience Platform, Adobe Real-Time CDP, Adobe Journey Optimizer, Adobe Target, Adobe Customer Journey Analytics and Adobe Workfront — together with the Adobe Experience Platform Agent Orchestrator.

What does an Adobe Experience Manager implementation partner do?

An AEM implementation partner designs the content architecture, builds and configures the AEM environment, migrates existing content and assets, integrates AEM with surrounding systems such as CDPs, CRM and commerce platforms, and then operates or supports the platform after go-live.

Which industries does Aventis deliver Adobe work for?

Primarily telecommunications operators, financial services and enterprise brands across Singapore, Indonesia, the Philippines and the wider Asia-Pacific region.

Can Aventis integrate Adobe Experience Cloud with existing telecom or banking systems?

Yes. We build bi-directional integration layers connecting Adobe Experience Platform to billing, CRM, charging, network event and data warehouse systems, using streaming infrastructure such as Kafka for real-time ingestion and activation.

Does Aventis provide managed services after go-live?

Yes. We provide ongoing AEP, AEM and campaign operations support — platform administration, release management, performance optimisation and continued use-case delivery after the initial programme completes.
