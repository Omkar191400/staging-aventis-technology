Common questions

Adobe & AEM, answered.

Is Aventis Technology an Adobe partner?
Yes. Aventis Technology is an Adobe Digital Experience Partner delivering Adobe Experience Cloud programmes for enterprises across Asia-Pacific, from architecture and implementation through migration, optimisation and always-on operations.

Which Adobe products does Aventis Technology implement?
Aventis delivers across Adobe Experience Platform, Adobe Real-Time CDP, Adobe Journey Optimizer, Adobe Customer Journey Analytics, Adobe Experience Manager, Adobe Target, Adobe GenStudio and Adobe Offer Decisioning. Programmes typically combine multiple capabilities into one connected experience architecture.

What does an Adobe Experience Manager implementation partner do?
An AEM implementation partner designs the content architecture, builds and configures the platform, migrates content and digital assets, and integrates AEM with surrounding systems such as customer data platforms, CRM and commerce platforms. The role can also extend beyond launch into platform operations, optimisation and ongoing support.

Which industries does Aventis deliver Adobe work for?
Aventis delivers Adobe programmes across telecommunications, financial services, aviation and travel, and retail and consumer businesses, with delivery experience across Singapore, Indonesia, the Philippines, Malaysia and India.

Can Aventis integrate Adobe Experience Cloud with existing telecom or banking systems?
Yes. Aventis integrates Adobe with the wider enterprise technology landscape, connecting customer data, identity, consent, CRM, analytics, messaging and other business systems. Its work includes real-time data foundations, journey orchestration and decisioning at enterprise scale.

Does Aventis provide managed services after go-live?
Yes. Aventis stays beyond implementation, providing always-on operations, platform administration, release management, performance optimisation, data-quality engineering and continuous use-case delivery. The team that builds the platform can continue to operate, tune and evolve it after go-live.
