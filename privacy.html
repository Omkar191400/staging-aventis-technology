1. Who we are

Aventis Technologies Pte. Ltd. (“Aventis”, “we”, “us” or “our”) is a company incorporated in the Republic of Singapore, with its registered office at 2 Venture Drive, #13-26, Vision Exchange, Singapore 608526.

We are a technology consulting and digital solutions partner. For personal data collected through this website and in the course of our business relationships, Aventis acts as the data controller.

2. Scope of this policy

This policy explains how we handle personal data relating to visitors to aventistechnology.com, prospective and existing clients, partners, suppliers and job applicants.

It does not cover personal data that we process on behalf of our clients when delivering services - for example subscriber data held inside a client's Adobe Experience Platform environment. That processing is governed by our contract with the relevant client and is described in Clause 12 below.

4. How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry and, where relevant, to scope and discuss a potential engagement.
  • To manage our contractual relationship with clients, partners and suppliers.
  • To assess job applications and manage recruitment.
  • To operate, secure and improve this website.
  • To understand, in aggregate, how the site is used - only where you have accepted analytics cookies.
  • To meet legal, regulatory and accounting obligations.

We do not sell personal data. We do not add website enquirers to marketing lists or newsletters without a separate, explicit opt-in.

5. Legal bases for processing

Where data protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on the following:

  • Legitimate interests - responding to business enquiries, running and securing our website, and managing supplier and partner relationships.
  • Performance of a contract - delivering services and administering agreements with clients.
  • Consent - analytics and non-essential cookies, and any optional marketing communications. You may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legal obligation - where we are required to retain or disclose information by law.

6. Cookies and analytics

When you first visit the site you will see a cookie banner offering Accept and Decline. Your choice is stored in your browser's local storage under the key aventis-cookie-consent so that we do not ask again on every page.

Strictly necessary storage is always active. This is limited to remembering your consent choice and is required for the site to work correctly. It is not used to track you.

Analytics and marketing cookies are only set if you accept. If you decline, non-essential cookies are not set.

To change your choice later, clear this site's data in your browser settings; the banner will then appear again on your next visit. You can also block or delete cookies through your browser at any time, though parts of the site may behave differently as a result.

7. Sharing and disclosure

We share personal data only where it is necessary, and only with parties bound to protect it:

  • Service providers - hosting, email, form handling and, where enabled, analytics providers acting on our instructions.
  • Professional advisers - legal, accounting and insurance advisers where relevant.
  • Group companies and delivery teams - our own personnel across the locations listed on this site, on a need-to-know basis.
  • Authorities - where disclosure is required by law, court order or a competent regulator.

We do not share your personal data with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

8. International transfers

Aventis operates across Singapore, Indonesia, the Philippines, India and Canada, and our service providers may process data outside your country of residence.

Where personal data is transferred across borders, we take steps to ensure it remains protected to a comparable standard - including contractual safeguards with recipients and, in respect of Singapore, compliance with the Transfer Limitation Obligation under the Personal Data Protection Act.

9. Retention

We keep personal data only for as long as it is needed for the purpose it was collected for:

  • Enquiries that do not progress - up to 24 months, so we have context if you come back to us.
  • Client and supplier records - for the duration of the relationship and then as required for legal, tax and audit purposes.
  • Job applications - up to 12 months after a decision, unless you ask us to remove them sooner.

When data is no longer needed, we delete it or anonymise it so it can no longer be linked to you.

10. Security

Aventis maintains an information security programme that is independently audited against the AICPA SOC 2 Type II standard, covering security, availability and confidentiality of the systems we use to deliver our services.

Controls include access management on a least-privilege basis, encryption of data in transit, monitoring and incident response procedures, and supplier due diligence. No system can be guaranteed completely secure, but we take these obligations seriously and review them regularly.

11. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask us to:

  • Confirm whether we hold personal data about you, and give you access to it.
  • Correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Delete information where we no longer have a valid reason to keep it.
  • Restrict or object to certain processing, including processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw consent where our processing relies on it.
  • Receive a copy of certain information in a portable format.

To exercise any of these, email privacy@aventistechnology.com. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law, and we may need to verify your identity first. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to your local data protection authority - in Singapore, the Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC).

12. Client and subscriber data

When delivering services, Aventis frequently processes personal data belonging to our clients' own customers - for example within Adobe Experience Platform, a customer data platform, or a pricing or feedback system we operate on a client's behalf.

In that context our client is the controller and Aventis acts as a processor (or, under the Singapore PDPA, a data intermediary). We process that data only on the client's documented instructions and under the terms of the applicable agreement and data processing addendum, which you can read more about on our Legal page.

If you are a customer of one of our clients and wish to exercise your rights over your data, please contact that organisation directly, as they determine how your data is used. We will support them in responding.

13. Third-party links

This site links to third-party services, including LinkedIn and Medium, where our published content is hosted. Those platforms have their own privacy policies and cookie practices, and we are not responsible for how they handle your data. We recommend reviewing their policies before interacting with them.

14. Children

Our website and services are directed at businesses and are not intended for children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our practices, technology or legal obligations. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised. Where changes are significant, we will take reasonable steps to bring them to your attention.
