Questions

Working with Aventis Technology.

Is Aventis Technology an Adobe implementation partner in APAC?
Yes. Aventis Technology is an Adobe Digital Experience partner headquartered in Singapore, delivering Adobe Experience Cloud implementations for enterprises across Asia-Pacific, including Singapore, Indonesia, the Philippines, Malaysia and India. Our work spans architecture, implementation, migration and always-on managed operations.
Which Adobe products does Aventis Technology implement?
We implement Adobe Experience Platform, Real-Time CDP, Journey Optimizer, Customer Journey Analytics, Experience Manager, Adobe Target, Offer Decisioning and GenStudio. Programmes typically combine several of these, with our engineers handling data architecture, identity and consent, journey design and activation across every channel.
Do you work with platforms other than Adobe?
Yes. While the majority of our delivery runs on Adobe Experience Cloud, we implement and integrate Braze, Salesforce, Confluent Kafka, Google Cloud and BigQuery, Twilio, Snowflake and Tableau. We also build custom digital products, including consumer apps, super apps and digital wallets.
What is hyper-personalization and how does Aventis deliver it?
Hyper-personalization means the content itself changes per individual, not just which segment receives which campaign. We deliver it by binding content templates to the live customer profile and to a real-time decision, so the layout is assembled at the moment of the request. A single managed component then renders a different offer, benefit set or message for every customer and every channel, with brand and eligibility guardrails enforced automatically.
Which countries and industries do you serve?
We serve enterprise clients across Asia-Pacific from our Singapore headquarters, with delivery experience in Indonesia, the Philippines, Malaysia, Singapore and India. Our deepest sector experience is in telecommunications, aviation and travel, financial services and retail, where customer bases run at national scale, in the hundreds of millions across our programmes.
Where are Aventis Technology's delivery centres?
We operate seven delivery centres: Singapore, Jakarta in Indonesia, Manila in the Philippines, Bangalore, Pune and Tirupati in India, and Toronto in Canada. The spread means core working hours cover more than twenty hours of every weekday, with managed services closing the remaining window on rotating shifts, so delivery and support follow the sun across Asia-Pacific and North America.
What does an Adobe Experience Platform implementation involve?
A typical programme starts with discovery and outcome definition, then moves through data architecture covering schema design, identity resolution, consent and governance, then platform build and integration, use case delivery in phased drops, and finally managed operations. Value usually lands within the first few months rather than at the end of a multi-year build.
Do you take over platforms implemented by another partner?
Yes. A significant part of our work is inheriting live platforms from an incumbent, stabilising them, and taking full ownership of operations, releases and continuous optimization. We run structured handovers with knowledge transfer, documentation and a defined ownership date.
What are Prezio and CX Pulse?
Prezio is our dynamic pricing and offer decisioning engine, which scores every customer against a full offer catalog and publishes a winning offer and price per channel, with commercial guardrails and holdout-based measurement. CX Pulse is our experience management platform, capturing NPS, CSAT and CES across every touchpoint with AI-read verbatims and closed-loop workflows.
